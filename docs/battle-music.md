# Battle music assets

The battle music selector plays these local MP3 files in `public/music/`:

- `pokemon-fire-red-battle.mp3` — [Pokémon FireRed/LeafGreen Trainer Battle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M3o5-Ny4eU0)
- `clash-of-clans-battle.mp3` — [Clash of Clans Combat Music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w9UKmLQq03A)
- `game-of-thrones-theme.mp3` — [Game of Thrones Main Theme (Extended)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AdQ3JDLlmPI)

The first two tracks were selected from the provided YouTube search results;
the third uses the provided video link. Music stays off by default. Check the
rights and platform terms for these recordings before distributing or deploying
them publicly.
