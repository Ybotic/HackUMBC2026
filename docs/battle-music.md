# Battle music assets

The battle music selector expects these MP3 files in `public/music/`:

- `pokemon-fire-red-battle.mp3`
- `clash-of-clans-battle.mp3`
- `game-of-thrones-theme.mp3`

The tracks are not included. Add only audio you have permission to use. Music
stays off by default, and choosing a track before its MP3 is added will show a
missing-file message.
